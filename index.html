P2R

Perception, Reasoning, Responding

Our mission is to understand how AI systems perceive natural language, perform multi-step reasoning, and generate responses grounded in that structure. We investigate gaps in current models’ ability to recover and utilize implicit causal, relational, and graphical structure, and develop methods to improve robustness and interpretability.

Our ultimate goal is to improve the ability of LLMs to reason in a manner comparable to humans, extending past basic pattern recognition to a more exhaustive visualization of relationships.

Research Areas

  • Causal Inference — Studying limitations in LLM causal reasoning and improving interpretability.
  • Model-Agnostic Architectures — Building reasoning frameworks that generalize across models and tasks.
  • Reasoning Visualization — Developing intermediate structured reasoning beyond plain text outputs.
  • Reasoning Faithfulness — Studying mismatches between reasoning steps and final outputs.
  • Human Intelligence & Reasoning — Understanding how humans reason and transferring insights to AI.
