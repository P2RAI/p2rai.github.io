Perception, Reasoning, Responding

AI Reasoning Research Initiative

P2R is an independent AI research initiative focusing on how AI systems perceive natural language, perform multi-step reasoning, and generate responses grounded in that structure. We investigate gaps in current models’ ability to recover and utilize implicit causal, relational, and graphical structure, and develop methods to improve robustness and interpretability.

Our long-term objective is to advance AI systems beyond pattern matching toward deeper forms of reasoning that more closely resemble the structured and relational thinking exhibited by humans.

Research Areas

  • Causal Inference — Studying the ability of language models to understand latent relationships and variables such as 2nd and 3rd variable structures from causality. Also includes understanding and development of Directed Acrylic Graphs. in complex environments.
  • Model-Agnostic Reasoning Frameworks — Constructing architectures and evaluation methods that generalize across models, domains, and tasks.
  • Reasoning Visualization — Investigating limitations in direct LLM inference and simpler prompting-based reasoning, and developing intermediate reasoning structure past basic textual output.
  • Reasoning Faithfulness — Understanding whether model-generated explanations accurately reflect the processes used to reach conclusions.
  • Human Intelligence & Reasoning — Studying differences between human and machine reasoning to identify opportunities for improving AI. In what ways do humans reason that AI cannot, and how can this knowledge be used to improve AI?

Current Focus

We are evaluating causal reasoning capabilities in large language models through benchmark design and structured reasoning analysis.

Vision

We believe future AI systems will require a richer understanding of relationships, structure, and causality. By investigating how models reason—and where they fail—we aim to contribute toward more reliable, interpretable, and capable intelligent systems.
