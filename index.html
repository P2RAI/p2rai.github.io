Perception, Reasoning, Responding

P2R Research Lab

P2R is an independent AI research initiative focused on understanding how intelligent systems perceive information, reason through complex relationships, and generate grounded responses. We investigate the mechanisms underlying reasoning in large language models and develop methods to improve robustness, interpretability, and causal understanding.

Our long-term objective is to advance AI systems beyond pattern matching toward deeper forms of reasoning that more closely resemble the structured and relational thinking exhibited by humans.

Research Areas

  • Causal Inference — Studying the ability of language models to identify causes, effects, confounders, and latent relationships in complex environments.
  • Model-Agnostic Reasoning Frameworks — Developing architectures and evaluation methods that generalize across models, domains, and tasks.
  • Reasoning Visualization — Exploring intermediate reasoning representations that provide more insight than direct textual outputs.
  • Reasoning Faithfulness — Investigating whether model-generated explanations accurately reflect the processes used to reach conclusions.
  • Human Intelligence & Reasoning — Studying differences between human and machine reasoning to identify opportunities for improving AI.

Current Focus

We are currently exploring causal reasoning benchmarks for large language models, with an emphasis on implicit graphical structures, multi-variable dependencies, and the distinction between correlation and causation.

Vision

We believe future AI systems will require a richer understanding of relationships, structure, and causality. By investigating how models reason—and where they fail—we aim to contribute toward more reliable, interpretable, and capable intelligent systems.
